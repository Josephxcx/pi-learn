Plant nutrition / Nutrient mobility

Read the plant,
starting with leaf age.

Where a symptom appears helps explain which nutrients the plant can move—and which it cannot readily redistribute.

Different tissue.
Different clues.

Select a part of the plant to connect its age with nutrient movement.

Older leaves can supply new growth.

Mobile nutrients such as nitrogen and magnesium can be redistributed from older tissue. When supply is limited, deficiency symptoms commonly appear in older leaves first.

Use location as a clue. Crop species, root health, disease, and growing conditions can produce overlapping patterns.
