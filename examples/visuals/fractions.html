Equal parts matter.

The denominator counts equal parts in the whole. The numerator counts selected parts. Dividing the numerator by the denominator gives the decimal value.

1 Divide

Partition the whole into four equal-sized pieces. Each piece is one quarter.

2 Select

Choose three pieces. Together they make three quarters; one quarter is left.

3 Connect

3 ÷ 4 = 0.75. Multiply by 100 to express the same quantity as 75%.
